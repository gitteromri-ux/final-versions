> People born the same year can be 33 years apart in biological age.
> 954 38 year old New Zealanders were tested to measure how old their bodies actually were. The youngest in the room was 28. The oldest? 61. Same birth year. Thirty-three years apart.

Source: [Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2018/04/5-healthy-habits-may-increase-life-expectancy-by-decade-or-more/). Headline must read "up to 14 years" (14 = women, 12 = men).

## 9. Blunt assessment of what went wrong here (so it isn't repeated)

- The agent cannot hear or watch video. Every quality claim was frame- and transcript-based. For a video task that is a structural gap, and it produced two rejected versions before the brand look was right.
- Time was lost to: generating a voice without an accent spec (one wasted take), a 30.04 s clip hitting a 30 s Extend limit (several failed attempts before uploading a trimmed file), and building cards from description instead of asking for the exact Canva references on day one.
- The fix for the next operator: get the exact brand references **before** the first render, specify accent in every prompt, keep one asset per card, and watch the output with eyes and ears before claiming anything.
